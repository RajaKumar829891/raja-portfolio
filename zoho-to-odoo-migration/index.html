Why companies move from Zoho to Odoo

  • One system instead of many. Odoo puts CRM, sales, inventory, accounting, manufacturing, HR and your website on one database, so there's no syncing between separate Zoho apps.
  • Flexible customisation. Odoo is built on an open Python framework, so workflows, reports and screens can be shaped exactly to your process.
  • Control over hosting and cost. Run Odoo Online, Odoo.sh or your own server, and choose Community or Enterprise depending on your needs.

What gets migrated

My Zoho to Odoo migration process

  1. Audit. Review your Zoho modules, custom fields and data quality, and agree what moves to Odoo.
  2. Map. A field-by-field mapping from Zoho to Odoo, including custom fields, stages, taxes and accounts.
  3. Clean. Remove duplicates, fix broken references and normalise formats before import.
  4. Test migration. Load everything into a staging copy of Odoo and validate record counts, totals and relationships with your team.
  5. Final run & go-live. A last delta migration of recent records, then switch-over and support while your team settles in.

Zoho to Odoo migration FAQ

What data can be migrated from Zoho to Odoo?

Typically contacts and companies, leads and deals, products, quotes and sales orders, invoices, bills, payments, chart of accounts and opening balances, plus notes, activities and attachments where needed. The exact list is agreed in the mapping phase.

Will we lose data when moving from Zoho to Odoo?

No. Records are mapped field by field, migrated to a test database first and checked with record counts and totals before the final run. My Zoho CRM and Zoho Books to Odoo 19 migration moved 100K+ records with zero data loss.

Can we keep using Zoho during the migration?

Yes. You keep working in Zoho while the migration is tested. A final delta run moves the latest records just before go-live.

Which Odoo versions do you migrate to?

Odoo 17, 18 and 19, Community or Enterprise, on Odoo Online, Odoo.sh or your own server.
