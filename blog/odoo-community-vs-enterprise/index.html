Odoo Community vs Enterprise: which should you choose?

One of the first decisions in any Odoo project is the edition. Both run on the same core, but they differ in licence, features, hosting and support. Here's a practical comparison from a senior Odoo developer who builds on both editions.

The short answer

Odoo Community is free and open source (LGPLv3). It covers CRM, Sales, Purchase, Inventory, Invoicing, Manufacturing, Project, Website and more, and suits many small and growing businesses, especially when paired with a developer who can fill gaps with custom modules.

Odoo Enterprise is a paid, per-user subscription on top of the same core. It adds extra apps and features, Odoo's official hosting options, version upgrades and support. It suits businesses that need full accounting, Studio, mobile apps or want Odoo SA to handle hosting and upgrades.

Key differences

Licence & cost

Community: free, open source. Enterprise: paid subscription, priced per user. Check Odoo's current pricing page for exact numbers in your country.

Accounting

Community includes invoicing and payments. Enterprise adds full accounting features such as bank synchronisation, advanced reporting and localisation packages.

Extra apps

Enterprise adds apps such as Studio, Helpdesk, Field Service, Planning, Sign, Payroll and more, plus the official mobile apps.

Hosting & upgrades

Community runs on your own server or Docker. Enterprise can also run on Odoo Online or Odoo.sh and includes official version upgrade services.

When Odoo Community is enough

  • You mainly need CRM, Sales, Purchase, Inventory, Invoicing, Manufacturing or Website.
  • You're comfortable self-hosting (or having a developer host it on Docker or a VPS).
  • You'd rather invest budget in a few targeted custom modules than in per-user licences.

When Odoo Enterprise is worth it

  • You need full accounting, payroll, Helpdesk, Field Service or Studio.
  • You want managed hosting on Odoo Online or Odoo.sh and official upgrades.
  • You run several companies or countries and want localisation packages and support from Odoo SA.

My recommendation

Start from your processes, not the edition. List what each team must do in Odoo, check which edition covers it as standard, and price the gap: licences on one side, custom development on the other. Community vs Enterprise FAQ

Can I upgrade from Odoo Community to Enterprise later?

Yes. Enterprise is built on the same core, so a Community database can be moved to Enterprise later. Custom modules may need small adjustments.

Is Odoo Community really free?

Yes. The software is free and open source under LGPLv3. You still pay for hosting, and for any implementation or development work you need.

Can a developer add Enterprise-like features to Community?

Often, yes. Many gaps can be filled with custom or community modules, but some Enterprise apps are large products in their own right, so compare the cost of building against the licence.
