How an Odoo implementation project runs

  1. Discover. Understand your processes, pain points, users and reporting needs.
  2. Plan. A written scope covering apps, edition (Community or Enterprise), hosting, customisations, timeline and cost.
  3. Build. Configure and develop on a staging database, with regular demos so you can give feedback early.
  4. Test & migrate. User acceptance testing, then migration of your live data.
  5. Go live & support. Switch-over, training and post-launch support, then new phases as you grow.

For larger businesses I recommend a phased rollout: for example CRM and Sales first, then Inventory and Purchase, then Accounting. Each phase goes live on its own, which lowers risk and gets value sooner.

Odoo implementation FAQ

How long does an Odoo implementation take?

A focused rollout of one or two apps can go live in a few weeks. Multi-company or heavily customised implementations take longer and are best delivered in phases.

How much does an Odoo implementation cost?

It depends on the number of apps, users, customisations and data to migrate. Working directly with me is typically up to 75% cheaper than a traditional ERP partner. I give a written scope and quote before work starts.

Do I need Odoo Enterprise, or is Community enough?

Many small businesses run well on Community. Enterprise adds features such as full accounting, Studio, mobile apps, Odoo.sh hosting and official upgrades. See my Community vs Enterprise guide or ask me to compare them against your processes.

Can you take over an implementation another partner started?

Yes. I can review the existing setup and custom modules, fix issues and continue the rollout.
