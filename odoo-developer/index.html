Odoo developer FAQ

How much does it cost to hire an Odoo developer?

It depends on scope. Working directly with a senior freelance Odoo developer typically costs up to 75% less than a traditional ERP partner because there is no agency overhead. Share your requirements for a fixed quote or an hourly estimate.

Should I choose Odoo Community or Odoo Enterprise?

Community is free and open source and suits many small businesses. Enterprise adds apps such as full Accounting, Studio, Odoo.sh hosting and official support. I help you compare them against your processes and budget before you commit.

How long does an Odoo implementation take?

A focused rollout of one or two apps can go live in a few weeks. Multi-company or heavily customised projects take longer and are best delivered in phases, one department at a time.

Can you work on an existing Odoo project or with our Odoo partner?

Yes. I can take over existing custom modules, fix bugs, upgrade modules to a newer Odoo version and add features, working alongside your team or partner.
